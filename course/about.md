## What does Aijutsu do?

Aijutsu solves three problems for organisations: a lack of senior technical direction, initiatives that need organisation-level coordination, and a lack of capacity to deliver internal or special projects. We offer three services.

### Technical advisory

Aijutsu provides senior technical opinion on a needs basis, for organisations that need it but don't need a full-time hire. We also offer advisory retainers.

### Transformation journeys

Aijutsu facilitates organisation-wide technology change: migrating to or away from cloud-native technologies, achieving ISO 27001 and SOC 2 compliance, and adopting AI effectively. We bring demonstrated industry experience and a coaching-informed methodology, so the change lasts well after the engagement ends.

### Bespoke development

Aijutsu builds software with AI-leveraged delivery: websites, e-commerce sites, internal tools, family bots, and fully featured platforms. Everything we ship is designed to be usable and maintainable.

## What makes Aijutsu different?

Aijutsu operates as builder-executives: leadership and management expertise, backed by hands-on engineering.

- **Organised around outcomes, not implementation.** Offshore practices mainly sell workload capacity, and boutique practices sell architecture expertise. We organise around the business outcome that the implementation is supposed to produce, and provide capacity only when it serves that outcome. You work with a senior operator, not a bench of juniors behind an account manager.
- **Experience in highly regulated domains.** Over a decade of delivery in government, cybersecurity, fintech, and cloud, including taking companies through ISO 27001 and SOC 2 Type II audits, and growing engineering teams from a few people to multi-regional.
- **AI-leveraged delivery.** Paired with that experience, AI lets a small practice ship production software in weeks instead of quarters.
- **Coaching-informed.** Our founder is a formally trained ontological coach, so your team levels up while we deliver, not in a handover meeting at the end.
- **The change outlasts us.** You keep the systems, the documentation, and the ability to run both.

In short: get in, do a good job, level your team up, get out.

## What does the name "Aijutsu" mean?

The name "Aijutsu" is the shortest version of our philosophy: three readings of "ai", sharpened by "jutsu".

| Character | Reading | Meaning | Principle |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 愛 | ai | Love | The love for craft and for people that runs through everything we make and everyone we meet. |
| AI | AI | Intelligence | Leveraging AI to accomplish more with less: smaller teams with outsized outcomes. |
| 居合 | iai | Decisiveness | The decisiveness and accuracy of a single well-timed cut: move once, move right, or don't move at all. |
| 術 | jutsu | Technique | Expertise as technique: a craft honed deliberately, never assumed finished. |

## How is Aijutsu involved with AI in the Heartlands?

Aijutsu created and maintains AI in the Heartlands, a hands-on course that helps everyday people use AI to build things for themselves and their communities.

The course applies the same principles we bring to client work. AI lets small teams achieve outsized outcomes, and AI in the Heartlands puts that capability in the hands of individuals and community groups. It is taught hands-on, so learners keep the skills after the course ends.

- **Free for self-learning.** Anyone may read the course materials and learn from them for free, under the [Terms of Use](./terms.md).
- **Workshops.** Aijutsu runs paid workshops based on the materials, where learners work alongside others and get help applying the concepts.
- **Licensing and group courses.** Teaching the materials, or reusing them in other course materials, needs written approval from Aijutsu. To license the materials, or to have Aijutsu run a course for your group, email [hello@aijutsu.dev](mailto:hello@aijutsu.dev).
