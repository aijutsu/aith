# Cleaning Up

This lesson removes what the course put on your computer. Use it when you are done with the course, or when you want to start again from a clean machine.

Nothing here is part of building your agent. Skip this lesson while you are still using NanoClaw.

## The steps

Each step has its own page:

| Step | What you do | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Uninstalling NanoClaw](./01-uninstalling-nanoclaw/index.md) | Run NanoClaw's own uninstaller, then delete its folder. | It removes the background service, the container, and the data of this copy of NanoClaw. |

## Before you remove anything

- **Removing NanoClaw is not undoable.** Your agent's memory and your saved keys go with it.
- **Your Telegram bot is not on your computer.** It lives on Telegram's servers, so removing NanoClaw leaves it there. The [Uninstalling NanoClaw](./01-uninstalling-nanoclaw/index.md) page says how to delete it.
- **The tools from [Getting Started](../01-installations/index.md) stay.** Git, Docker, Make and Codex are normal tools that other work uses. Remove them the way you would remove any other app, if you want to.
