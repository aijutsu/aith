# Agent playtime

So far Louis has been talking to you alone. This lesson puts him where he belongs: in a group chat with other people, doing the job.

Nothing new gets installed here. Everything happens in Telegram and Notion, with the agent you already have.

Before you start, finish [Setting up data sources](../03-setting-up-data-sources/index.md). Louis must be answering you and writing to your Notion page.

## The steps

Each step has its own page:

| Step | What you do | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Create a Telegram group](./01-create-telegram-group/index.md) | Make a group and put your bot in it. | A community agent needs a community to sit in. |
| [Add your agent to the group](./02-add-agent-to-group/index.md) | Connect Louis to that group, then give him work. | The bot is the phone line. Louis is who answers it, and connecting them is a step of its own. |
| [Add humans to the group](./03-add-humans-to-group/index.md) | Invite neighbours, and see what each of them may do. | Louis treats a stranger, a verified neighbour and an admin differently. This is where that shows. |
| [Update your agent's personality](./04-update-personality/index.md) | Change how he writes, and make it stick. | The template is a starting point, not the finished agent. |

## What you'll see along the way

- **Louis writes things down while you chat.** Every answer that matters lands in your Notion page, and you can read it there.
- **He checks who is asking.** Not by name — by the account the message came from.
- **He asks before he posts.** Being allowed to do something and being told to do it now are two different things to him.
