# Copy the Louis template into Notion

Your agent is called **Louis**, and Notion is where he keeps everything: who is in your community, what is on, who has borrowed what. He does not build that structure himself. It comes ready-made, as a template you copy into your own [Notion](../../../glossary.md#notion) workspace.

Copying it is this page. It takes a minute.

## Copy it

1. Open the template: <https://app.notion.com/p/Louis-the-Community-Builder-3e4f2896ef0c80988978ee9724337b93>
2. Sign in to Notion if it asks you to.
3. Click **Duplicate**, at the top right.
4. Choose **your own workspace**. If you belong to more than one, pick the one you want your community to live in. You can't move it easily later.

Notion makes your own copy and opens it. From here on, this copy is yours: Louis writes to it, and nobody else's copy is touched.

## What you just copied

One page, with sixteen [databases](../../../glossary.md#database) inside it. You don't have to read them all now — Louis knows what each one is for — but it is worth knowing what is there:

| These hold | Databases |
| --- | --- |
| Who is in the community | Members, Telegram Accounts, Discord Accounts |
| Where they live | Blocks |
| What's happening | News, Events |
| Things people share | Items for Loan, Loans |
| Neighbours helping neighbours | Help Requests, Introductions, Lost and Found |
| The neighbourhood itself | Neighbourhood Directory, Community Cats, Cat Sightings, Estate Issues |
| What Louis did, and why | Admin Log |

They are empty, and that is normal. They fill up as you and your neighbours talk to Louis.

> [!NOTE]
> The template is built for a Singapore HDB or BTO estate, so it talks about blocks, Town Council, HDB and NEA. It works anywhere: a "block" can be a building, a street, or a cluster of houses, and you can rename the reporting options to suit your own place.

## Check that it worked

You should have:

- a page in **your** workspace, in the sidebar on the left; and
- sixteen databases inside it.

> [!WARNING]
> Keep working in **your copy**, not the template you copied it from. They look identical, and Louis will ask you for the link to your copy on the last page of this lesson. If you send the link to the original by mistake, Louis ends up writing into the wrong place — or finds he can't write at all.
