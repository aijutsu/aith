# Cleaning up

This lesson removes what the course put on your computer. Use it when you are done with the course, or when you want to start again from a clean machine.

Nothing here is part of building your agent. Skip this lesson while you are still using [NanoClaw](../../glossary.md#nanoclaw).

## The steps

Each step has its own page:

| Step | What you do | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Uninstalling NanoClaw](./01-uninstalling-nanoclaw/index.md) | Run NanoClaw's own uninstaller, then delete its folder. | It removes the background service, the container, and the data of this copy of NanoClaw. |
| [Uninstalling the tools](./02-uninstalling-tools/index.md) **(optional)** | Remove the tools from Getting started, and the accounts and services the course set up. | Only if you want your computer back the way it was. Other programs may use these tools. |

## Before you remove anything

- **Removing NanoClaw is not undoable.** Your agent's memory and your saved keys go with it.
- **Your [Telegram](../../glossary.md#telegram) bot is not on your computer.** It lives on Telegram's servers, so removing NanoClaw leaves it there. The [Uninstalling NanoClaw](./01-uninstalling-nanoclaw/index.md) page says how to delete it.
- **The tools from [Getting started](../01-installations/index.md) stay, unless you remove them.** [Git](../../glossary.md#git), [Docker](../../glossary.md#docker), [Make](../../glossary.md#make) and [Codex](../../glossary.md#codex) are normal tools that other work uses. [Uninstalling the tools](./02-uninstalling-tools/index.md) removes them, in the right order, if you want to.
