# Concept recap

You have built a working community agent. Until now, each page showed you one step at a time. This lesson steps back and shows you the whole picture.

Nothing new gets installed here, and you don't have to change anything. Some pages ask you to open a file and look inside, so keep your [terminal](../../glossary.md#terminal) and [Telegram](../../glossary.md#telegram) nearby.
