# Customising your agent

So far Louis has been talking to you alone. This lesson puts them where they belong: in a group chat with other people, doing the job.

Nothing new gets installed here. Everything happens in [Telegram](../../glossary.md#telegram) and [Notion](../../glossary.md#notion), with the agent you already have.

Before you start, finish [Setting up data sources](../03-setting-up-data-sources/index.md). Louis **must already be answering you** and writing to your Notion page or the next steps won't work.

## The steps

Each step has its own page:

| Step | What you do | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Create a Telegram group](./01-create-telegram-group/index.md) | Make a group and put your bot in it. | A community agent needs a community to sit in. |
| [Add your agent to the group](./02-add-agent-to-group/index.md) | Connect Louis to that group, then give them work. | The bot is the phone line. Louis is who answers it, and connecting them is a step of its own. |
| [Add humans to the group](./03-add-humans-to-group/index.md) | Invite neighbours, and see what each of them may do. | Louis treats a stranger, a verified neighbour and an admin differently. This is where that shows. |
| [Update your agent's personality](./04-update-personality/index.md) | Change how they write, and make it stick. | The template is a starting point, not the finished agent. |
| [Schedule a message](./05-schedule-a-message/index.md) | Have them post in the group every 5 minutes, then switch it off. | A community agent speaks up on its own too: a morning summary, a weekly look ahead. |

## What you'll see along the way

- **Louis writes things down while you chat.** Every answer that matters lands in your Notion page, and you can read it there.
- **They check who is asking.** Not by name — by the account the message came from.
- **They ask before they post.** Being allowed to do something and being told to do it now are two different things to them.
- **They can work on a clock.** A scheduled task wakes them up with nobody asking.
